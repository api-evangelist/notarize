A lender closes a loan using one RON platform. Months later, that loan is sold to an investor whose due diligence team wants to confirm the notarization holds up to scrutiny. That's the exact problem MISMO's RON Certification exists to solve, and it's why "is this platform MISMO certified" is one of the first questions serious mortgage professionals ask before choosing a RON vendor.
